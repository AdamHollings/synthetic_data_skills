# Speech and clinical audio

## Applies to

Consultation audio, doctor–patient dialogue, transcripts with matching audio, non-speech clinical
sounds. For written text only, use `clinical-text.md`. This modality is reasonably covered for
normal speech, with one significant gap.

Separate the two jobs before choosing a tool: generating the dialogue, and rendering it to audio.
Many PoCs need only the first.

## Tools

| Tool | Use for | Verdict | Constraints |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SDialog (`sdialog.github.io`) | Persona-driven multi-agent dialogue (Doctor/Patient personas), structured JSON export, matching audio synthesis with room-acoustics simulation. MIT licence | Use | Should link to any available LLM. Audio synthesis may pull heavier dependencies — check |
| NoteChat pattern (arXiv `arxiv.org/abs/2310.15959`, ACL Anthology) | Cooperative doctor/patient LLM agents conditioned on clinical notes; the dataset also documents methodology | Inspiration | Not a library — implemented with your own agent framework |
| Bark (`github.com/suno-ai/bark`) | Speech plus non-speech events: coughs, sighs, pauses | No verdict recorded — confirm with user | GPU strongly recommended; slow inference; model download |
| pyttsx3 / eSpeak | Offline TTS, no deep learning, few lines of code | No verdict recorded — confirm with user | Robotic quality; placeholder audio only, not realism-sensitive PoCs |
| Hugging Face TTS models | Range of text-to-speech models | Maybe | Large model downloads; environment integration unresolved |
| Coqui | Named in the audit's gap assessment as covering normal speech | Unassessed | No row, link, setup notes or verdict recorded |

SDialog covers dialogue and audio together, and its JSON export yields a transcript aligned to the
audio, which supplies ground truth without transcription. Use Bark when a non-speech event is
itself the clinical signal.

## Inspiration entry

### NoteChat

arXiv `arxiv.org/abs/2310.15959`, ACL Anthology. Read the source before reimplementing; written from the abstract
on 2026-07-29, and the full pipeline detail is in the PDF. A dataset and a method, not a library.

- **Pattern:** an ensemble of role-specific LLM agents (doctor, patient) using structured
  role-play and strategic prompting to generate dialogue conditioned on a clinical note.
- **Copy:** role-specific agents rather than one model writing both sides — the paper's claim is
  that assigned roles are performed more effectively this way. Condition the conversation on a
  structured artefact rather than a free prompt.
- **Substitute:** the models, and the role set (nurse, carer, interpreter, family member).
- **Don't inherit:** nothing to install.
- **Caveats:** evaluated on MTS-dialogue against ChatGPT and GPT-4 with reported gains up to
  22.78% for models trained on their dialogues — a claim about their dataset, not a guarantee for
  ours. Dialogue realism claims rest on their human evaluation, which we cannot inherit; if
  consultation realism matters, plan clinician review. For a speech PoC, the transcript is
  LLM-generated, so any label must come from the patient state, not the conversation.

## Gaps

- **Pathological and abnormal speech**: dysarthria, post-stroke, Parkinsonian, paediatric voices.
  Not generated by anything in the audit. This is what diagnosis-from-speech PoCs require —
  synthesising healthy speech and labelling it dysarthric produces a dataset with no signal. State
  it is unsupported and confirm the approach with the user.
- Accents, code-switching and interpreter-mediated consultations are not covered.
- Audio privacy auditing is not addressed by any tool in the audit. Voice is biometric; raise it if
  audio may leave the environment.

## Label-validity trap

If an LLM generates the dialogue and supplies the diagnosis or triage label predicted from speech,
the result is uninformative. Derive the label from the patient state before generating dialogue.
See `from-intelligence.md`.

## Coherence

- Generate dialogue from the patient state and the encounter it belongs to (`healthcare-ehr.md`),
  including what the patient would not yet know at that point in the timeline.
- Keep the transcript as ground truth and audio as its rendering. Preserve SDialog's JSON
  alignment rather than transcribing audio back.
- One voice per synthetic person across encounters; consistent clinician voice within a department.
- A note and a recording of the same encounter are generated from the same state. A symptom in the
  note that is never voiced in the audio is a coherence failure.
